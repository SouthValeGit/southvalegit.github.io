FAQ

What exactly is free? Modules 0 to 6, the 7-step overview, a script template and 5 prompts. Free forever, no sign-up.

Do I need to show my face? No. These are faceless, animated story channels.

Do I need to code? No. Only the optional automation module (Module 9) uses code, and the scripts are included.

Which tools do you use? Roblox Studio or Blender for the world and animation, CapCut or DaVinci Resolve for editing, and AI tools for scripts, voice and captions. Everything has a free option to start.

Pro Package or Studio Membership? Take the Pro Package if you want the full method once. Take the membership if you also want new scripts, breakdowns and feedback every month.

Will I make money? Nobody can honestly promise that. Instagram and Facebook only pay for original content, which is why the course teaches you to make your own videos. Results depend on your format, consistency and effort.

Refunds? Pro Package: if it is not what was described, message us within 14 days. Studio Membership: cancel anytime and you will not be charged again.
